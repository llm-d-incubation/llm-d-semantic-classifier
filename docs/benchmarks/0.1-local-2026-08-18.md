## 1. Inference floor by input length (single request, no cache, no network)

200 measured requests per row after 20 warmups.

| tokens | p50 | p90 | p95 | p99 | max | req/s |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 11.86ms | 12.40ms | 12.58ms | 13.00ms | 13.06ms | 85 |
| 64 | 15.22ms | 15.93ms | 16.21ms | 16.70ms | 22.48ms | 65 |
| 128 | 23.35ms | 24.59ms | 25.15ms | 26.00ms | 32.14ms | 42 |
| 256 | 51.85ms | 53.76ms | 54.72ms | 55.69ms | 59.97ms | 19 |

Tail is tight: p99 within ~10% of p50 at every length. Cost scales sub-quadratically
but faster than linearly (8x tokens -> 4.4x latency).

## 2. Concurrency scaling (64-token inputs, shared resident classifier)

| concurrency | reqs | p50 | p95 | p99 | max | aggregate req/s |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 60 | 15.5ms | 16.7ms | 18.2ms | 18.2ms | 64 |
| 2 | 120 | 15.6ms | 16.6ms | 17.3ms | 18.5ms | 128 |
| 4 | 240 | 17.5ms | 18.6ms | **20.0ms** | 21.3ms | 227 |
| 8 | 480 | 26.3ms | 29.8ms | 31.2ms | 32.4ms | 302 |

Linear to concurrency 2, still favourable at 4, past the knee at 8 (throughput
+33% for +50% latency). **Operating point: concurrency <= 4** — which is what the
bounded executor (AC-008 / ADR-0002) enforces, and why admitting more should be
rejected rather than queued.

## Interpretation

A hit is ~4 orders of magnitude cheaper than a miss (~1us vs 15.5ms at 64 tokens),
so the hit rate governs mean latency:

| hit rate | mean latency (64-token miss path) |
|---:|---:|
| 50% | ~7.8ms |
| 90% | ~1.6ms |
| 99% | ~0.16ms |

**Assessment against the ~20ms uncached objective:** met at 32 tokens (11.9/13.0
p50/p99) and 64 tokens (15.2/16.7); NOT met at 128 (23.4) or 256 (51.9) on CPU.
p99 at concurrency 4 lands exactly on 20.0ms. The service is viable for routing
at <=64-token inputs with concurrency capped at 4; longer inputs need truncation,
batching, or a GPU.
